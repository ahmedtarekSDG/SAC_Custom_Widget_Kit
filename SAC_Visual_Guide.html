Decision ladder

Climb from the left and stop at the first step that answers the business question. A custom widget gives up story features (theme, commenting, scheduling, translation, Search to Insight), so it has to earn its place through an encoding, density or interaction that SAC lacks, or through reuse across many stories.

1Native widgetThe visual exists in the chart picker, including Sankey, gauge and funnel since QRC3 2026.
2CompositeA reusable group of native widgets (numeric point + chart + text) for headers and tiles. Each inner widget runs its own query.
3Widget add-onCustom tooltip or drawn overlay on bar/column, stacked, line, area or numeric point.
4Custom widgetNew encoding, higher density (one query instead of six) or a new interaction. Start from the kit.
5Vendor extensionIBCS suites, Gantt or map products with support, licence and hosting.

Visual gallery

Grouped by the question the visual answers, following the nine relationships of the Financial Times Visual Vocabulary plus business-specific forms. Examples use illustrative figures in m SAR.

KPI card types

Thirteen tile patterns, from the bare number to the exception board. Rules for all of them: never a number without a reference; name the comparison window; sign and colour variances by favourability, not by sign; carry status in an icon or word as well as colour; units in the title; five to seven cards per row at most; every card leads somewhere when clicked.

Comparison guide

Pick the visual by what you compare. For two values per category the three common charts carry three different messages: a dumbbell encodes the change as the length of a bar (gap size), a slope chart as the tilt of a line (direction and rank swaps), grouped bars as two lengths from zero (absolute values).

Build backlog for the kit

Effort: S ≈ 1–2 days, M ≈ 3–5 days, L ≈ 1–2 weeks, including harness tests and documentation. Every build keeps the kit standard: data binding with linked analysis, image export for PDF/PPT, theme awareness, favourability and scenario properties, keyboard focus and sentence-style accessible labels.

Recipes with the current kit

No new code: bind the measures shown and paste the JSON into the Universal Chart's optionOverride property. The override is merged element by element into the generated ECharts option, so series[1] changes the second generated series.
